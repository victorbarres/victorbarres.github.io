  2. Realistic with respect to what?
  3. What’s in the measurand? Model, or model plus harness?

  5. Who maintains it?

What evaluation is for

Six jobs, each judged differently. Training sits beside them, judged on learnability.

FunctionWho it servesJudged on
PredictDeployersDoes the ranking match deployment outcomes?
CompareResearchers, regulatorsFairness, reproducibility, stability over time
PrescribeThe ecosystemDoes it encode what we actually value?
SignalBuilders, new entrantsDoes it show that a capability or market exists?
ExposeModel builders, data providersAre the failures real, reproducible, actionable?
LearnPractitioners, the curiousDoes it show how agents actually work and fail?
