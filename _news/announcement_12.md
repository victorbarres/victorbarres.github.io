Talk at Training Agents IRL (GitHub, San Francisco; hosted by Hugging Face / OpenEnv): _Fit for Purpose: Environments, benchmarks, and the metrology of AI agents_ ([slides](/assets/html/fit-for-purpose-training-agents-irl-2026.html), [video](https://youtu.be/CKFUx7COIwQ?t=1170)). Attach a task and a grader, and an environment produces a number. You can judge it by what it does to the model, or by what it tells you about the model: a reward is not a measurement.
