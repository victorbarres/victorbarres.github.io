New paper with Aden Barton, and with [Nicolai J. Foss](https://www.cbs.dk/en/research/departments/department-strategy-and-innovation/nicolai-j-foss) and [Henrik M. Nielsen](https://www.cbs.dk/en/research/departments/department-strategy-and-innovation/henrik-moller-nielsen) of Copenhagen Business School, building a bridge between organization theory and AI evaluation: [_Harder Than the Task_](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03494) ([blog](https://www.mercor.com/blog/playing-well-with-others/)). Benchmarks are made of tasks, but a job is done with others, who hold information, make decisions and depend on you. Only 16% of benchmarks measure any organizational dimension, though attention is growing fast: from 14% claiming one in 2023 to 33% in 2026.
