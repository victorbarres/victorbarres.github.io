Two new voice-agent benchmarks with Soham Ray and the Sierra team, with leaderboards on [taubench.com](https://taubench.com). [τ-Entity](https://sierra.ai/blog/tau-entity-can-voice-agents-get-the-details-right) starts from something that sounds trivial: getting someone's name, phone number or email right over the phone. When agents do check a wrong value, only 24–37% get repaired. [τ-i18n](https://sierra.ai/blog/tau-i18n) takes the τ-bench airline, retail and telecom tasks into Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Hindi, Korean and Mandarin, and scores task completion, interaction and generation separately. They don't line up.
